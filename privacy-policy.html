Privacy Policy: Dungeon Tavern

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Dungeon Tavern ("the game", "the app") is published by Prefreeze ("we", "us"). You can reach us at nulltoken.gaming@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the game uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It covers the Android app com.dungeontavern.myapp.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no analytics of its own, and no servers of ours. We do not collect your personal information, and we never sell it.
  • Your game progress lives on your phone.
  • A few optional features use Google services (Play Games, AdMob ads and Play Billing). Those services handle some data under Google's own policies, described below.

What stays on your device

  • Game progress and settings. Your tavern, staff, upgrades, achievements, settings and a record of what you have bought are stored in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.
  • Backups. The game keeps a rolling backup and a snapshot of your save on the device so it can recover from a damaged file. These never leave the phone.
  • Android backup. If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include the game's data in your phone's backup to your Google account. That is Google's feature, controlled in your phone's settings, and we cannot see it.
  • Problem list. If the game runs into an error, it keeps a short entry (the error message, roughly where it happened, and the time) under Settings > Problems. This stays on your phone. It is only sent if you choose to: the "Email it" button opens your email app with the report ready to send, and nothing is sent until you press Send in that app. You can also clear the list at any time.
  • Notifications. Reminders (for example, a pantry running low) are scheduled on your phone by the game itself. They are not sent from a server. You can switch them off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Optional features that use Google services

None of these are required to play. Each is handled by Google under its own terms, and we receive none of this data ourselves.

  • Cloud save (Google Play Games). If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy of your save is stored in your Google account through Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it on a new phone. You can delete it from the Play Games app or your Google account. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Leaderboards (Google Play Games). These are off until you switch them on in the Shop tab. If you do, your best scores (Legacy earned, best single run, renown, trophies found) are sent to Google Play Games along with your Play Games player name and player ID. Leaderboards give no rewards. You can switch them off at any time. To remove scores you have already submitted, use the Play Games settings.
  • Rewarded ads (Google AdMob). An ad is shown only when you tap to watch one. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information, as described in Google's advertising policy. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are shown a consent message before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying the Supporter pack means you never need to watch an ad.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing). Payments are processed by Google Play. We only learn that a purchase was completed, such as which pack you own. We never see your payment details.

Share codes and exports

  • Share my tavern code. This text contains only your tavern name, rank, Legacy earned, trophies found and location. It is created only when you tap the button, and goes only where you choose to paste or send it.
  • Export save. This creates a block of text you can copy to keep your game safe. It goes only where you put it.

Permissions

The app asks for these Android permissions:

  • Internet, so ads, Play Games and purchases can work.
  • Notifications, to show the reminders you have switched on.
  • Advertising ID, which ad services use as described above.
  • Vibration, for the touch feedback you can switch off in Settings.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

How long data is kept

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Data held by Google (Play Games saves and scores, advertising data, purchase records) is kept under Google's policies, and you can manage or delete it through your Google account.

Your choices and rights

You can play without signing in to anything. You can switch off leaderboards, notifications and vibration in the game, decline ad consent where asked, reset your advertising ID in Android, and delete your save by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, and to object to some uses of it. Because we do not collect your data, the requests that matter are with Google; you can make them through your Google account. If you contact us at the address above, we will help however we can.

Security

Your game data stays on your device, protected by Android's own app sandbox. Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone's own security up to date.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the game can show ads, it is intended for ages 13 and over. If you believe a child has used the game and given personal information to one of the Google services above, contact us and we will help get it removed.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its date before the change reaches you. If a future version adds a new service, such as a crash-reporting tool, this policy will be updated first.
